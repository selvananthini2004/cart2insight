# Olist E-commerce – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes the Olist e-commerce dataset to understand sales, customers, orders, sellers, payments, delivery performance and customer reviews.

The analysis uses SQL Server to retrieve data and Python libraries such as Pandas, Matplotlib and Seaborn to analyze and visualize the results.

### Objectives

- Understand order and sales performance.
- Identify customer purchasing patterns.
- Analyze delivery performance and shipping costs.
- Evaluate seller and product-category performance.
- Understand customer satisfaction.
- Identify areas where the business can improve.

## 2. Analysis Performed

### 2.1 Univariate Analysis

Univariate analysis examines one variable at a time.

The notebook analyzes:

- Order status distribution
- Year-wise and monthly order counts
- Customer distribution by state and city
- One-time and repeat customers
- Year-wise sales
- Sales by product category and state
- Freight value distribution
- Delivery delay distribution
- Orders per seller
- Seller distribution by state
- Payment methods and installments
- Customer review ratings

**Major findings**

- Most orders in the dataset are delivered (96,478 orders).
- SP has the highest customer count and sales value.
- Most customers are one-time customers (96.88%).
- Credit card is the most commonly used payment method (75.24%).
- Five-star reviews account for 57.78% of reviews.

### 2.2 Bivariate Analysis

Bivariate analysis studies the relationship between two variables.

The notebook analyzes:

- Delivery delay vs review score
- Delivery status vs review score
- Product price vs freight value
- Seller order count vs seller revenue
- Product category vs review score
- Review count vs average review score
- Customer order count vs customer spending
- Repeat customer type vs average spending

**Major findings**

**Delivery delay vs review score**

- Observation: Five-star reviews have an average delivery delay of -13.39 days, while one-star reviews have an average of -4.06 days.
- Interpretation: Higher review scores are associated with deliveries arriving earlier compared with the estimated delivery date.
- Business Impact: Improving delivery reliability and delivery-date estimates may help improve customer satisfaction.

**Repeat customers vs average spending**

- Observation: Repeat customers have average spending of 310.49, compared with 161.49 for one-time customers.
- Interpretation: Repeat customers spend more on average in this analysis.
- Business Impact: Customer retention activities may help increase customer value and encourage additional purchases.

**Seller order count vs revenue**

- Observation: Sellers with different order counts can generate similar revenue.
- Interpretation: Revenue depends on factors such as product value and product mix, not only order volume.
- Business Impact: Seller performance should be evaluated using both order count and revenue.

### 2.3 Multivariate Analysis

Multivariate analysis examines multiple variables together.

The notebook analyzes:

- Delivery status, review score and delivery days
- Average delivery delay and customer satisfaction
- Seller state and customer state
- Average freight value and delivery days by route
- Correlation between review score and delivery measures

**Major findings**

- Observation: Delivery duration and delivery delay vary across review-score groups.
- Interpretation: Delivery experience is one useful factor to investigate when understanding customer satisfaction.
- Business Impact: Monitor delivery time, delivery status and review score together to identify service issues.

- Observation: Freight cost and delivery duration vary across seller-to-customer state routes.
- Interpretation: Shipping performance differs depending on the route.
- Business Impact: Identify routes with high freight costs or long delivery times and investigate possible logistics improvements.

### 2.4 Trend Analysis

Trend analysis studies how business performance changes over time.

The notebook analyzes monthly:

- Delivered order count
- Total sales
- Average order value
- Average delivery delay
- Average review score

**Major finding**

- Observation: Monthly order count and sales change over the period, with higher activity in several months of 2017 and 2018.
- Interpretation: Monthly trends help identify changes in demand and business performance.
- Business Impact: Use monthly sales and order trends for demand planning, inventory planning and operational capacity decisions.

## 3. Overall Business Findings

1. **Sales:** Sales increased from approximately 6.16 million in 2017 to 7.39 million in 2018.
2. **Customers:** The customer base contains a high percentage of one-time customers.
3. **Retention:** Repeat customers have higher average spending than one-time customers.
4. **Delivery:** Approximately 91.89% of classified delivered orders were delivered early, while 8.11% were delivered late.
5. **Customer satisfaction:** More than half of the reviews are five-star ratings.
6. **Payments:** Credit cards are the most frequently used payment method.
7. **Geography:** São Paulo is the leading state in customer count, seller count and sales.
8. **Logistics:** Freight costs and delivery duration vary across geographic routes.

## 4. Business Recommendations

- Improve customer retention through loyalty programs and relevant product recommendations.
- Investigate late deliveries and identify the reasons for delays.
- Monitor low-rated products and categories to understand customer complaints.
- Evaluate sellers using revenue, order count, delivery performance and review scores.
- Review high-cost shipping routes and identify opportunities to reduce freight expenses.
- Use monthly sales trends to support inventory and demand planning.

## 5. Conclusion

The analysis provides an overview of Olist's sales, customer behavior, seller performance, delivery operations and customer satisfaction.

The key opportunities identified are improving customer retention, monitoring late deliveries, understanding low customer ratings and optimizing shipping routes.

By monitoring these business areas together, the company can make better decisions about sales growth, customer experience and logistics performance.
